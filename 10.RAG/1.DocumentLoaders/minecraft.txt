In a world made square beneath cubic skies,
Where the dawn awakes and the sun doth rise,
I awaken bare on a sandy shore,
With an open world and a wooden door.

First punch the bark of an oak so tall,
Watch the cube of wood to the grass blades fall.
Craft a crafting bench on the dirt and stone,
In this pixel realm where I stand alone.

From a wooden pick to the cobble grey,
I must carve a shelter before the day
Fades into dusk and the stars shine bright,
For the shadows crawl with the fall of night.

Hear the distant groan of a zombie near,
And the rattling bones that awaken fear.
Draw a bow of string as the arrows fly,
Underneath the dome of a midnight sky.

Hissing through the dark comes a green surprise,
With a vacant stare in its hollow eyes.
Tread with cautious feet or the ground will tear,
Leaving craters deep in the frightened air.

When the morning breaks with its gentle gleam,
Walk along the bend of a crystal stream.
Shear the woolly sheep for a bed of white,
Never fear again the approaching night.

Digging deep below to the caves obscure,
Where the glowing ores in the dark allure.
Iron, coal, and gold in the cavern's vein,
Through the winding paths of the stone domain.

Lava pools ignite with a fiery glow,
Casting crimson light on the deeps below.
Careful with the stride on the edge of slate,
Lest the molten sea shall decide your fate.

Hear the drop of water, the bat's swift wing,
Hear the iron pick make the bedrock sing.
At the level deep where the slate turns cold,
Lies a prize far greater than gleaming gold.

Glinting azure blue in the solid wall,
Diamond blocks await at the miner's call.
Forge the azure blade and the armor grand,
Now a fearless knight in a blocky land.

Build a portal frame out of dark obsidian,
Strike the flint and steel with a swift precision.
Purple swirling mist opens up the gate,
To the fiery hell where the piglins wait.

Crimson forests sway in the sulfur breeze,
Nether fortresses towering over seas.
Blazes shoot their fire with a fiery spin,
Collect the golden rods for the craft within.

Brewing stands arise with a bubbling sound,
Potions strange and swift from the depths unbound.
Eyes of Ender fly to the sky ahead,
Leading to the place where the ancients tread.

Find the fortress deep underneath the clay,
Where the portal room keeps the light at bay.
Place the final eyes in the ring of stone,
Step across the void to the dark unknown.

Pillars made of black in a starless gloom,
Where the dragon circles the silent doom.
Shoot the crystals down till the cages burst,
Quench the ancient sky of its wicked thirst.

With a final roar and a blinding light,
Falls the winged queen of the endless night.
Read the scrolling words as the journey ends,
In a universe that the mind transcends.

Back to humble fields and the golden wheat,
Where the friendly cows and the villagers meet.
For the world is vast and the craft is free,
An eternal land of creativity.
